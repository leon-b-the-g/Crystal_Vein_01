# Kit.txt: which bought asset stands in for which piece of the deck.
# The game reads this when it starts: change a line, save, Play.bat. No build, and the editor may stay open.
#
#   Piece = asset     the piece is drawn with this mesh, scaled to fill the piece's grey box and standing where the box stood
#   Piece + asset     the mesh stands on top of every piece of that kind, at its own size; several lines take turns, tile by tile
#   Piece + none      ... and with this line a share of them stays bare
#   Piece @ Room = asset   and   Piece @ Room + asset: the same, in that room only (the room's name as the game shows it).
#                     Where a room has lines of its own, they go before the lines for every room.
#   WallFace + asset  a panel on every side of a wall that looks into a room: the mesh must be modelled lying flat; it is stood up
#                     and stretched to one tile wide and the wall's height. Several lines take turns; @ Room works here too.
#   Creep + asset     what the growth is made of. These also swell in, tile by tile, as the growth spreads.
#   alien = asset     an animated body (a skeletal mesh) for one of the sim's aliens: crawler and mite are in this section;
#                     possessed, brute, spitter, stalker exist. Its animations are found in the same pack by their names:
#                     Idle, Walk, Run, Attack, Damage or Hit.
#
# Pieces for "=":  Floor Wall Door DoorLocked Crate Barrel Console Table Locker Vent Corpse Item Cell Flare NoiseBox
# Pieces for "+":  Floor Table Crate Barrel Console Locker Creep (a tile with a terminal, the generator or the cell rack stays clear)
# The asset: in the editor's content browser right-click it, Copy Reference, and paste it here. /Game/Folder/Name does as well.
# After the asset, if needed:   scale=1.5   yaw=90   z=10 (cm up)   glow=cold | amber | off (the colour of the mesh's own lights;
# works for the sci-fi kit. Colour rule: cold is the ship, amber is the ship asking for attention, red is alien and nothing else.)
# Furniture turns its back to the wall it stands at by itself; yaw= turns a mesh that then looks the wrong way.
# A room is furnished by what the level puts in it (tables, crates, lockers, a console: the plan in the sim's story module) and by
# the lines here that say what those are in that room: a table is a workbench in the workshop and a desk in the office.
# A # in front of a line switches it off and the grey box is back. A line whose asset is not on this PC is skipped;
# the log says so (Saved/Logs/CrystalVein.log, lines starting "Kit.txt").
# What a pack holds, as pictures: README, "Bought assets".
